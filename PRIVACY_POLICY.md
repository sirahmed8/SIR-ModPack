## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### 2.1 Sovereign Offline-First Architecture
**SIR ModPack Ecosystem** (SIR ModPack) is engineered with an offline-first privacy paradigm. Core application operations, databases, cache indices, and project files reside 100% locally on your workstation storage. We do NOT harvest, monitor, or transmit your private files, source code, database tables, or sensitive documents to central servers without your explicit, voluntary action.

### 2.2 Categories of Processed Data
Under this application, processed data is strictly limited to:
- **Operational Data**: Launcher instance preferences, allocated RAM limits, custom screen resolutions, and player skin cache. Zero tracking of private player chat.
- **Technical Telemetry**: Application version, operating system environment, error diagnostics (only upon user-approved crash reports).
- **Billing Telemetry**: For paid subscriptions, cryptographic invoice tokens handled by certified payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers.
